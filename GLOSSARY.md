# Gwydion Portfolio

Gwydion's personal website: it presents him to employers first, to prospective freelance clients second, and hosts his writing. This glossary captures terms whose meaning is specific to this site and easy to confuse.

## Language

### Audiences

**Recruiter** (de: _Recruiter_):
Anyone evaluating Gwydion for employment: in-house recruiters, hiring managers, engineers reviewing a candidate. The primary audience; when audiences conflict, the Recruiter wins.
_Avoid_: employer, HR, visitor (when the audience matters)

**Client** (de: _Auftraggeber_):
Anyone considering hiring Gwydion for freelance work. A secondary audience.
_Avoid_: customer, lead

**Reader** (de: _Leser_):
Anyone who comes for Gwydion's writing rather than to hire him.
_Avoid_: subscriber, follower

### Content

**Profile** (de: _Profil_):
The single set of facts about Gwydion himself: who he is, his pitch, his skills, his Availability and what he is doing now.
_Avoid_: bio, about (as a thing; About is a page)

**Availability** (de: _Verfügbarkeit_):
What Gwydion is currently open to, as shown to Recruiters and Clients: for example "open to full-time roles, plus selected freelance projects".
_Avoid_: status (alone), open to work

**Project** (de: _Projekt_):
A piece of Gwydion's work presented as evidence of what he can do: his own products, client work, open source. Each Project has its own page telling its story.
_Avoid_: case study (that is the page, not the thing), portfolio item, work item

**Career entry** (de: _Station_):
One stage of Gwydion's path: a job, a freelance period, an education. Career entries together make up the CV.
_Avoid_: job, position, experience item

**CV** (de: _Lebenslauf_):
The downloadable summary of Gwydion's Career entries and skills, aimed at Recruiters.
_Avoid_: résumé

**Post** (de: _Beitrag_):
A piece of Gwydion's writing, published under Writing. Written in exactly one Post language and reachable at exactly one address, whatever the Site language.
_Avoid_: article, blog entry, note

**Overview** (de: _Übersicht_):
The site's home: a dashboard of tiles summarising the Profile, featured Projects, latest Posts and Availability.
_Avoid_: home page, landing page, dashboard (in copy)

### Languages

**Site language** (de: _Seitensprache_):
The language the site's interface is shown in: English or German. Every page exists in both Site languages.
_Avoid_: locale (in conversation), UI language

**Base language** (de: _Basissprache_):
English: the Site language that interface text is written in first and that the other falls back to.
_Avoid_: default language, source language

**Post language** (de: _Beitragssprache_):
The one language a Post is written in, English or German. Independent of the Site language: every Post is listed in both Site languages and marked when its Post language differs.
_Avoid_: translation, locale
